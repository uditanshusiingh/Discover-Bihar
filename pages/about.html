DISCOVER THE STORY
THE IDEA

Bihar has always had a story to tell.

Discover Bihar was created as a digital heritage exploration project to bring together the many dimensions of Bihar in one immersive experience. Instead of looking at heritage as something frozen in the past, the project presents history, culture, food, art, people and travel as parts of a living story.

WHY DISCOVER BIHAR

From ancient knowledge to living culture.

Bihar is home to places and traditions that connect different chapters of Indian history — from the ancient learning centre of Nalanda and the Buddhist heritage of Bodh Gaya to traditional crafts, festivals and regional cuisine.

This website brings those experiences together in a simple, visual and accessible format designed for students, travellers and anyone curious about Bihar.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Preserve the past.
Experience the present.
Inspire the future.

Heritage becomes meaningful when it can be understood, experienced and shared. Discover Bihar aims to present Bihar's stories in a way that feels relevant to today's digital generation while respecting the richness of the traditions behind them.
