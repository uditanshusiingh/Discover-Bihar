STORIES BEHIND THE CRAFT

Creativity passed through generations.

Each tradition carries more than technique. It carries memory, symbolism and a connection between people and place.

01 MITHILA · PAINTING · SYMBOLISM

Madhubani Art

A visual language where stories become patterns.

Madhubani, or Mithila painting, is associated with the Mithila region and is recognised for strong outlines, vibrant colours and recurring motifs.

Traditional themes may draw from mythology, nature, social life and auspicious occasions. Different styles and communities have contributed to the tradition over time.

Region Mithila
Expression Painting
Motifs Nature & Stories
02 TEXTILE · EMBROIDERY · STORYTELLING

Sujuni Embroidery

Threads that turn everyday fabric into visual storytelling.

Sujuni is a hand-embroidered textile tradition associated with Bihar. Designs are created through repeated stitches and carefully composed motifs.

Contemporary practitioners have also used Sujuni as a medium for expressing themes of village life, social experience and changing community identities.

Medium Textile
Technique Hand Stitching
Character Storytelling
03 GRASS · CRAFT · MITHILA

Sikki Craft

Golden grass transformed into objects of everyday beauty.

Sikki craft uses a special golden-coloured grass traditionally shaped into containers, toys, baskets and decorative objects.

The craft demonstrates how local materials can become highly detailed objects through patient handwork and inherited techniques.

Material Golden Grass
Region Mithila
Method Handcrafted
