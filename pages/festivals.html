BEYOND THE CELEBRATION

Stories behind the rituals.

Every festival carries memories, symbols and traditions passed from one generation to another.

01 DEVOTION · SUN · WATER

Chhath Puja

A festival of gratitude where riverbanks become places of devotion.

Chhath is marked by disciplined rituals, fasting and offerings made to the setting and rising Sun. Families and communities gather around water bodies for the ceremonies.

The sight of illuminated ghats, traditional songs and carefully prepared offerings makes Chhath one of Bihar's most visually distinctive cultural experiences.

Symbol Sun & Water
Setting River Ghats
Season Autumn
02 FAIR · TRADE · CULTURE

Sonepur Mela

A historic fair where commerce meets celebration.

Sonepur Mela takes place near the confluence of the Ganga and Gandak rivers and has long been associated with trade and pilgrimage.

Today, the fair is also known for performances, handicrafts, local food and the atmosphere of a large traditional gathering.

Location Sonepur
Setting River Confluence
Experience Fair & Culture
03 FAMILY · FAITH · TRADITION

Jitiya

A deeply personal tradition rooted in family and prayer.

Jitiya, also known as Jivitputrika Vrat, is observed by many women with rituals and fasting associated with the wellbeing of their children.

Like many living traditions, its expression can vary between communities and regions while retaining its strong family focus.

Theme Family Wellbeing
Tradition Fasting
Identity Living Culture
04 MITHILA · FOLK CULTURE

Sama-Chakeva

Folk tradition, songs and sibling affection come together.

Sama-Chakeva is associated with the Mithila region and is traditionally observed through songs, clay figures and community gatherings.

The celebration reflects the richness of Mithila's folk traditions and the importance of relationships within everyday cultural life.

Region Mithila
Expression Folk Songs
Theme Sibling Bond
