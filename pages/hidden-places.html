LOOK CLOSER

Every hidden place has a story.

Go beyond the photograph and discover what makes these landscapes part of Bihar's cultural identity.

01 FOREST · WILDLIFE · RIVER

Where Bihar turns wild.

Valmiki Nagar sits in a landscape where forests, rivers and the Himalayan foothill region create a markedly different atmosphere from the plains of central Bihar.

The Valmiki Tiger Reserve is the state's major tiger reserve and an important part of Bihar's natural heritage.

Find a nature experience
02 WATER · NATURE · ESCAPE

Follow the sound of falling water.

Kakolat Waterfall drops through a rocky, forested setting in Nawada district and has long attracted visitors looking for a natural retreat.

Its surroundings make it especially suited to a slower day away from the city's usual rhythm.

Find a nature experience
03 HILLS · HERITAGE · SPIRITUALITY

Walk through ancient landscapes.

Rajgir's hills are inseparable from the history of the ancient city. The surrounding landscape is associated with Buddhist and Jain traditions as well as the history of Magadha.

Here, natural scenery and historical memory exist almost side by side.

Find a heritage experience
04 SUFI HERITAGE · ARCHITECTURE

Discover a quieter spiritual Bihar.

Maner is known for its historic Sufi shrines, particularly the Maner Sharif complex, and for its connection to the cultural history of the region around Patna.

It offers a different perspective on Bihar's layered religious and architectural heritage.

PLAN YOUR ESCAPE

Know when to go.

Different landscapes reveal different sides of Bihar throughout the year. For most outdoor exploration, the cooler months offer a more comfortable experience.

October — March Generally comfortable for outdoor exploration and sightseeing.
OCT — NOV Fresh & Pleasant

A good period for exploring outdoor destinations.

DEC — FEB Cool & Clear

Comfortable temperatures for sightseeing.

MAR — MAY Warmer Days

Plan outdoor activities around cooler hours.

MONSOON Green Landscapes

Waterfalls and greenery can become especially dramatic, subject to local conditions.
