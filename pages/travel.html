Travel with curiosity.

Bihar rewards travellers who slow down and look beyond the obvious. From Buddhist heritage and ancient universities to forests, festivals and local food, every journey can take a different path.

SAMPLE JOURNEY

Three Days. Many Stories.

A sample route connecting Bihar's sacred, historic and cultural highlights.

01
DAY ONE

Bodh Gaya

Sacred Bihar

Begin the journey in one of Bihar's most important spiritual landscapes, where history, Buddhism and living pilgrimage come together.

  • Visit the Mahabodhi Temple complex.
  • Explore the surrounding monastic and pilgrimage areas.
  • Spend time around the Bodhi Tree and temple precinct.
  • Experience the quieter atmosphere towards evening.
SUGGESTED FOCUS Spirituality · History · Reflection
02
DAY TWO

Nalanda + Rajgir

Ancient Magadha

Travel deeper into Bihar's ancient intellectual and historical landscape, connecting the ruins of Nalanda with the hills and heritage of Rajgir.

  • Explore the archaeological remains of Nalanda Mahavihara.
  • Continue towards the historic landscape of Rajgir.
  • Discover the hills, ancient sites and surrounding heritage.
  • Slow down and take in the landscape before evening.
SUGGESTED FOCUS Knowledge · History · Landscape
03
DAY THREE

Patna

The Capital Story

End the journey in Patna, where centuries of history meet the energy of a modern Bihar city.

  • Explore Golghar and its historic surroundings.
  • Discover museums and cultural spaces across the city.
  • Spend time along the Ganga riverfront.
  • Finish the journey with Bihar's local flavours.
SUGGESTED FOCUS City Life · Culture · Food
TRAVEL SMART

Small details. Better journeys.

Check Dates

Festival dates and local events can follow traditional calendars, so check current dates before planning around them.

Check Timings

Monument, museum and attraction timings can change. Confirm current opening information before visiting.

Travel Light

Comfortable clothing, suitable footwear, water and essential travel items make heritage exploration easier.

Travel Respectfully

Respect religious spaces, local customs, communities, heritage sites and the environments you visit.
