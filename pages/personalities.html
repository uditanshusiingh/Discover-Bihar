01 LEADERSHIP · FREEDOM · SERVICE

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

A life shaped by scholarship, public service and the freedom struggle.

Born in Ziradei in present-day Bihar, Rajendra Prasad became an important figure in India's freedom movement and participated in the country's constitutional journey.

He went on to serve as the first President of the Republic of India. His association with Bihar remains central to the region's modern political and historical memory.

Known For First President
Field Public Service
Legacy National Leadership
02 NUMBERS · STARS · KNOWLEDGE

Aryabhata

An ancient Indian scholar whose mathematical ideas travelled across time.

Aryabhata is traditionally associated with Kusumapura, an ancient centre of learning generally identified with present-day Patna.

His work in mathematics and astronomy became an important part of the intellectual history of classical India, especially through his celebrated mathematical and astronomical treatise.

Field Mathematics
Centre Kusumapura
Era Classical India
03 STRATEGY · POLITICS · SCHOLARSHIP

Chanakya

A celebrated thinker associated with the political world of ancient Magadha.

Chanakya, also known as Kautilya or Vishnugupta in traditional sources, is remembered as a scholar and political strategist connected with the formation of the Mauryan state.

His name is closely associated with the Arthashastra, a foundational text on governance, economics, diplomacy and political strategy.

Also Known Kautilya
Field Statecraft
Tradition Ancient Magadha
04 FAITH · COURAGE · IDENTITY

Guru Gobind Singh

Patna Sahib preserves the memory of one of Sikhism's most important figures.

Guru Gobind Singh was born in Patna in 1666 and spent part of his early childhood in the city. The birthplace is commemorated by Takht Sri Patna Sahib.

As the tenth Sikh Guru, his life, teachings and leadership hold enduring importance in Sikh history and connect Patna with a major spiritual tradition.

Born Patna
Tradition Sikhism
Legacy Spiritual Leadership
